What AI did today

AI is moving stupid fast, and nobody can keep up by scrolling all day — so I keep a radar. Here’s the short version: what’s new, what to watch out for, and what genuinely blew my mind. Meta first, because that’s the world I live in.

ai-radar — daily brief / SUN · OCT 18, 2026 · EDITION 001 FEED SYNCED LATEST BRIEF
TL;DR — THE 20-SECOND VERSION Meta just had its biggest AI week ever. Its flagship model went open-weight, a brand-new frontier model landed, an AI coding agent opened for business — and the apps already in your pocket quietly got a lot smarter.
MINDBLOWINGMETA AIOCT 15, 2026

Muse Spark Open: Meta gave away the crown jewels

Meta released Muse Spark as an open-weight model — 816 billion parameters, a million-token memory, free for anyone to download and run. And the plot twist nobody saw coming: they teamed up with Apple to make it run beautifully on Mac Studios. Meta and Apple. Collaborating. I know.

WHY IT MATTERSThe most powerful Muse model isn’t locked behind an API anymore — anyone, anywhere, can build on it.
NEWMETA AIOCT 12, 2026

Muse Wonder: the new frontier model

Wonder is Meta’s top-tier model, built to go head-to-head with the best in the world. Two tricks stand out: Active Memory — it remembers you across chats — and Active Focus — it keeps pushing through blockers instead of giving up.

WHY IT MATTERSThis is where personal AI is heading: less goldfish, more actual assistant.
NEWMETA AIOCT 2026

Muse Code: an AI that writes the code

Meta’s coding agent, now a subscription from $50–$200/month. It writes, reviews, and helps ship code — the kind of help that used to require a whole engineering team on call.

WHY IT MATTERSIf you build anything — sites, scripts, side projects — this is worth a serious look.
NEWIN YOUR POCKETTHIS WEEK

WhatsApp’s Meta AI got a glow-up

Voice conversations, automatic chat summaries, and a writing assistant — right inside WhatsApp. No new app, no setup. It’s just there, in the chats you already have.

WHY IT MATTERSThis is how AI actually reaches everyone: quietly, inside the apps people already use every day.
WATCHMETA AITHIS WEEK

Facebook “Your Algorithm”: your feed, in your words

Instead of guessing what the algorithm wants, you just describe it in plain words — “more small-town food spots, less outrage bait” — and your feed adjusts.

WHY IT MATTERSIt cuts both ways: your feed becomes exactly what you ask for. So ask carefully.
