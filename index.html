01ANTHROPICOCT 19, 2026

Anthropic is giving away 5% of itself

Anthropic announced it’s putting 5% of its shares into a new public benefit fund — for nonprofits, direct payments to people, and help for workers affected by AI. The catch: stockholders still have to approve it, and the rules for how the money gets spent aren’t written yet. Elon Musk answered on X that he’ll hand one SpaceX share to each of America’s roughly 72 million kids next year.

WHY IT MATTERSBig AI labs are feeling real pressure to show they care about who gets hurt by this tech. Judge the pledge by the governance they publish, not the percentage.

Disney is rescuing most of Character.AI’s team

Character.AI filed for bankruptcy last week, and Disney is moving in — job offers for 60+ employees (most of the company), a $20M bid for some of its tech, and the startup’s old CEO is now Disney’s first CTO. The collapse came from burning cash plus serious legal trouble around young people using the chatbots.

WHY IT MATTERSEven the biggest AI chatbot app can fail when the business model doesn’t work. But Big Entertainment just decided AI talent is worth paying for.

04DEEPSEEKOPEN SOURCEOCT 19, 2026

DeepSeek just dropped a dirt-cheap coding model

DeepSeek V4.1-Flash is out — open weights under an MIT license, scoring 77% on the DeepSWE coding benchmark. The wild part is the price: about $0.15 per million input tokens, roughly one-fortieth of what Claude costs. They also launched a new Search API that undercuts Brave and Kagi by about 10x.

WHY IT MATTERSFrontier-level coding help is getting cheap fast. If you build with AI, your costs keep dropping.

05AGENTIC AIOCT 18, 2026

The internet is locking AI agents out

Amazon has been blocking Meta’s Muse since September. Yelp only lets in agents that pay for data. Delta won’t make deals with agent companies. JPMorgan and Charles Schwab block agents from touching customer accounts. The pattern: websites don’t want bots doing things in your name without their say-so.

WHY IT MATTERSThe dream of “my AI books everything for me” is running into a wall of locked doors. Agentic AI only works if the sites let it in.

The big three labs quietly teamed up on safety

OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google DeepMind revealed they’ve been coordinating for weeks on AI safety — joint cyber-focused models, shared safeguards, and access programs. These are companies that compete on everything else, so this is genuinely unusual.
