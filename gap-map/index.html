THE GAP MAP

Every campus in America. One dot. One gap.

LENSES
What am I looking at? The gap is the difference in 8-year award rates between students who received a Pell Grant and students who didn’t — one dot per campus, colored by that campus’s own gap. Every rate is recomputed from federal counts, for campuses where both groups had at least 50 students. Averages hide campuses: the national gap is 16.4 points; the median campus gap is 7.7. One map, many lenses — new lenses land only when their numbers reconcile like these did.
